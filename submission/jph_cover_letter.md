# Cover letter draft: Journal of Public Health

Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript, **“Socioeconomic and Geographic Inequalities in Anaemia Among Women of Reproductive Age in Ghana,”** for publication as an Original Paper in the *Journal of Public Health*.

Using nationally representative 2022 Ghana Demographic and Health Survey data, we examine the distribution of anaemia across the socioeconomic ranking and variation between communities. The analyses combine complex-survey prevalence estimates, the Erreygers-corrected concentration index, inequality decomposition, survey-weighted logistic regression and multilevel heterogeneity measures.

The manuscript extends recent Ghanaian prevalence and regression analyses by quantifying wealth-related inequality across the socioeconomic ranking. We estimate its measured domain contributions and examine geographic and residual community-level heterogeneity. Anaemia prevalence was approximately 41%, the Erreygers index indicated a disproportionate burden among poorer women, and important regional and community variation persisted after adjustment.

The findings are relevant to public-health monitoring because national averages can conceal socioeconomic and geographic differences in anaemia. Monitoring these differences may inform the allocation of additional support alongside population-wide prevention.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and uses de-identified secondary survey data obtained under The DHS Program's data-use conditions.
