Verified against the current Oxford Academic instructions on **2026-10-08**.

## Article setup

- **Article type:** Original Paper
- **Title:** Socioeconomic and Geographic Inequalities in Anaemia Among Women of Reproductive Age in Ghana
- **Running title:** Inequalities in Anaemia Among Women in Ghana
- **Abstract:** use `submission/jph_abstract_200_words.md`
- **Keywords:** Anaemia; women of reproductive age; socioeconomic inequality; concentration index; decomposition analysis; multilevel modelling; Ghana

The current Original Paper limits are **2,000–3,000 words** and no more than **4 tables/figures combined**. The current package satisfies both limits.

## Files to prepare for upload

1. **Title page**
   - Source: `submission/jph_title_page.md`
   - Must include the corresponding-author email before upload.
   - JPH asks for each author's surname/initials, designation, department, institution, city with postal code, and country.

2. **Main manuscript**
   - Source: `submission/jph_main_manuscript.md`
   - Preferred journal format is Word or RTF.
   - Use one-and-a-half spacing.
   - Do **not** add page numbers.
   - The abstract should appear on the second page and contain only the structured abstract.

3. **Tables**
   - Table I: `results/table1_weighted_characteristics.md`
   - Table II: `results/table2_full_adjusted_model.md`
   - Tables should remain editable.

4. **Figures**
   - Figure 1: concentration curve
   - Figure 2: regional prevalence
   - Upload the locally generated high-resolution files.
   - Alt text is already included directly beneath each figure legend in the manuscript.

5. **Supplementary material**
   - Source: `supplementary/supplementary_material.md`

6. **Cover letter**
   - Source: `submission/jph_cover_letter.md`
   - JPH requires an accompanying cover letter.

7. **Reporting guideline**
   - Observational study: STROBE
   - Source mapping: `submission/strobe_mapping.md`
   - Upload the official STROBE checklist if the portal requests one.

## Submission-form declarations

### Originality

The manuscript is original and should be submitted only to this journal while under consideration.

### Data availability

Use the manuscript's Data Availability statement. The DHS microdata are third-party restricted data available from The DHS Program following application and approval; raw DHS data are not redistributed in the repository.

### Funding

**No external funding was received.**

### Conflict of interest

**The author declares no conflict of interest.**

### AI disclosure

Use the existing disclosure in both the manuscript and cover letter:

OpenAI ChatGPT was used to assist with code development, statistical workflow documentation and manuscript drafting and language editing. All analytic decisions, numerical outputs, interpretations and final manuscript text were reviewed and verified by the author, who takes full responsibility for the work.

JPH explicitly requires disclosure of AI use when AI has been used for content generation, code, data processing, translation, or similar assistance.

### Open access choice

Open access is optional. If open access is **not** selected after acceptance, JPH states that the article will be published under standard subscription access and no open-access charge will be applied.
