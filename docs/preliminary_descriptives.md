# Preliminary Descriptive Profile

These figures are validation outputs from the Ghana 2022 DHS Individual Recode audit. They are not yet the final survey-analysis tables.

## Analytic sample

- Women with valid anaemia classification: **7,557**
- DHS sampling clusters represented: **618**
- Sampling strata represented: **32**
- Weighted prevalence of any anaemia: **41.12%**

## Selected weighted prevalence estimates

### Residence

- Poorer: **41.8%**

### BMI category

## Interpretation at audit stage

The preliminary weighted summaries show clear socioeconomic, geographic, reproductive, and nutritional gradients in anaemia. These estimates justify proceeding with the prespecified inequality and multilevel analyses.

Formal inference will use the complete DHS survey design, including weights, primary sampling units, and strata. These audit summaries should therefore not be treated as final manuscript estimates until survey-standard errors and confidence intervals are calculated.
