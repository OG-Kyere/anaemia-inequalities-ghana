## Overall prevalence

The survey-weighted prevalence of any anaemia was **41.12% (95% CI 39.60%–42.64%)**.

## Notes

- BMI categories exclude seven women with unavailable or flagged BMI information.
- Weighted percentages may differ from unweighted sample percentages because of the DHS sampling design.
- Estimates are descriptive and should not be interpreted as adjusted associations.
