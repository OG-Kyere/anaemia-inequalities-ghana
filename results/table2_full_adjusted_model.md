# Table 2. Survey-Weighted Adjusted Associations With Anaemia

Outcome: any anaemia versus no anaemia.

Analytic sample: **7,550 women** with valid anaemia measurements and complete BMI information.

The model adjusted simultaneously for age group, education, wealth quintile, residence, pregnancy status, parity, BMI category, employment, marital status, and region.

Sampling weights were applied, and standard errors were estimated using the DHS strata and primary sampling units.

## Interpretation

After simultaneous adjustment, pregnancy status, BMI category, and region show the strongest evidence of association with anaemia.

The crude wealth and education gradients attenuate substantially after adjustment. This should be interpreted together with the concentration-index analysis: socioeconomic inequality can remain present at the population level even when conditional wealth coefficients are close to the null.

These estimates are associations and should not be interpreted causally.
